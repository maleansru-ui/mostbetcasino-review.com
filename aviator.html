Strategy

How to play Aviator

Aviator runs on an RNG, so no strategy guarantees profit. What you can control is risk, and that is where the difference is made:

  • Set auto cash out. A multiplier of 1.5x or 2x delivers steadier results and removes the temptation to wait.
  • Start small. Low stakes let you learn the rhythm before committing real money.
  • Use the dual bet. Close one early to secure the stake, let the second run for a higher multiplier.
  • Avoid impulsive decisions. Play to a plan and stay inside the budget you set.
  • Manage the bankroll. Never stake more than 2–5% of your balance on a single round.
Remember: rounds are independent. Five low multipliers in a row do not make a high one more likely next time. Any system built on that assumption — martingale included — fails against the table limit.
Feature

Auto bet and auto cash out

Auto bet repeats the same stake every round; auto cash out closes the bet at a chosen multiplier. Running both is the simplest way to keep discipline through a long session, because the decision is made in advance and emotion never enters the round.

A practical setting: auto cash out between 1.5x and 2x, stake fixed. Doubling after a loss empties a balance remarkably fast once you account for the maximum bet limit.
