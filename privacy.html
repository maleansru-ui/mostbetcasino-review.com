Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This policy explains what data is collected when you visit mostbetcasino-review.com, why we process it and what rights you have. Our approach is simple: we collect the bare minimum.

1. What we collect

1.1. Data you provide

If you use the contact form you give us a name, an email address, a subject and the content of your message. These are used solely to reply to you.

1.2. Data collected automatically

  • IP address, normally anonymised by the analytics tooling.
  • Browser type, operating system and language.
  • Pages visited, time on site and referral source.
  • Device type and screen resolution.

This information is aggregated and statistical: it does not identify you personally.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We never ask for or store financial data, card numbers or banking credentials.
  • We do not request identity document numbers.
  • We have no access to your account or balance on the operator's platform.
  • We collect no special-category personal data of any kind.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Answering enquiries sent through the contact form.
  • Analysing use of the Site to improve its content and performance.
  • Detecting and preventing abusive or fraudulent use.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal basis

Processing rests on your consent (contact form and non-essential cookies) and on the legitimate interest of keeping the Site operational and secure.

5. Retention

Messages received through the contact form are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, and no longer than twelve months. Aggregated analytics data is retained according to the tool's own retention periods.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or transfer personal data. We may share strictly necessary information with:

  • Web analytics providers, which process aggregated and anonymised data.
  • The Site's hosting provider, acting as a data processor.
  • Competent authorities, where there is a legal obligation.

7. External links

The Site links to the operator's platform and to other third-party resources. Once you follow such a link you are subject to that site's privacy policy, over which we have no control. We recommend reading it before providing any data.

8. Cookies

We use technical and analytics cookies. Full details are in our cookie policy, which also explains how to disable them in your browser.

9. Security

The Site is served over encrypted HTTPS and we apply reasonable technical measures to protect information. No system is completely secure, so we reduce risk by limiting how much data we collect in the first place.

10. Minors

The Site is intended solely for people aged 18 or over and we do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover that we have received information from a minor, we delete it immediately.

11. Your rights

You may at any time exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw any consent you have given.

12. Changes to this policy
