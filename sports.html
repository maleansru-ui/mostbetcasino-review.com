Virtual sports

Virtual sports are software simulations that resolve every few minutes, independent of the real calendar. Football cups, horse and greyhound racing and cycling run around the clock, with outcomes determined by a certified RNG.

They fill the gaps when nothing live is on, but treat them for what they are: pure chance with no form to analyse.

Value

Odds and margin

On top football leagues the margin sits around 1.5–3%, which puts Mostbet in the upper half of the market. Smaller competitions and very specific markets carry a wider margin, as they do at every bookmaker.

A quick way to measure it on a 1X2 market: add the inverse of each price. If the total comes to 1.03, the margin is 3%. The closer to 1.00, the better the value for you.

Odds format: decimal, fractional and American are all available in the profile settings. Decimal is the default in most of the markets covered here.

Good practice

Betting sensibly

  • Set a monthly budget and never exceed it.
  • Stake a fixed 1–3% of your bankroll per selection.
  • Do not chase losses by doubling the next bet.
  • Specialise in a few competitions you genuinely follow.
  • Compare odds — a sustained 3% difference decides long-run results.
  • Keep a record of your bets to learn which markets actually work for you.
