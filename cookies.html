Cookie policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This policy describes which cookies mostbetcasino-review.com uses, what they are for and how you can manage or delete them from your browser.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a site stores on your device when you visit. Cookies remember preferences, measure use of the site and improve navigation. They are not programs: they cannot execute code or access other files on your device.

2. Cookies we use

3. Third-party cookies

Some external services — mainly web analytics tools — may set their own cookies when you visit. These are governed by the privacy policies of their respective providers.

When you click a link to the operator's platform, that platform may set its own cookies in your browser. That processing is outside our control and subject to the operator's cookie policy.

4. How to disable cookies

You can block or delete cookies from your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
  • Android / iOS: Browser settings → Privacy.

You can also browse in incognito or private mode, which discards cookies when the window closes.

Note: blocking all cookies can affect parts of the site, such as remembering your chosen language between visits.

5. Updates to this policy

We may amend this policy when the cookies in use or the applicable rules change. The current version is always the one published on this page, with its last-updated date.
