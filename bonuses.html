Bonus FAQ

What is the minimum deposit for the welcome bonus?
It depends on your market — typically the equivalent of $5–$10 for the cash bonus. The 250 free spins usually require a larger first deposit, so check the exact figure for your country.
Why did I only get 100% instead of 125%?
The 125% rate applies only if the first deposit is made within 30 minutes of registration. After that window the standard offer is 100%, valid for 7 days.
How does the 60x casino wagering work?
You must turn over the bonus amount 60 times before it converts to withdrawable cash. A $100 bonus therefore needs $6,000 of qualifying bets. Slots normally contribute 100%; table games and live dealer contribute less or nothing at all.
Can I hold two bonuses at once?
No. Only one bonus can be active on the account. You have to clear or cancel the current one before claiming the next.
What happens if I cancel an active bonus?
You lose both the bonus funds and any winnings generated from them. Only your own deposit balance remains.
Do free spins expire?
Yes. They are credited in batches and each batch has a limited validity window, usually 24–72 hours. Unused spins are forfeited.
