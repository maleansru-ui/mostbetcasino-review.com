Before you play

Reading RTP and volatility

RTP is the share of stakes a game returns over the long run. 96% means that across millions of rounds the game pays back 96 of every 100 units wagered. It is not a per-session promise: short-run results deviate heavily in both directions.

Volatility describes how that return is distributed. Low volatility pays small amounts often; high volatility pays rarely but larger. On a limited bankroll, low or medium volatility stretches your playing time far further.

  • Favour titles above 96% RTP.
  • Keep each spin to 1–2% of your balance.
  • Set a loss limit and a win target before the session starts.
  • If you are clearing a bonus, check how each category contributes.
