Cryptocurrencies

Mostbet supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, Binance Coin and Litecoin. This is the fastest withdrawal route by a wide margin — transactions usually confirm within an hour of approval, against 1–3 business days for cards.

Questions

Payments FAQ

What payment methods does Mostbet accept?
It varies by country. Every market gets local rails — UPI and Paytm in India, bKash and Nagad in Bangladesh, Pix in Brazil, Papara in Turkey, Humo and Uzcard in Uzbekistan — plus Visa, Mastercard and cryptocurrencies everywhere.
What are the minimum and maximum limits?
Minimums usually start around the equivalent of $5–$10 depending on the method, with local wallets typically cheaper to enter than international cards. Maximums are set per method and per market.
Does Mostbet charge fees?
Mostbet does not add its own commission, but the payment provider may. Check the final amount before confirming, and note that crypto networks charge their own variable fee.
How long do withdrawals take?
Crypto is fastest, usually clearing within an hour of approval. Cards and e-wallets take 1–3 business days. Verification must be complete before the first payout.
Can I withdraw to a different method than I deposited with?
Where possible you should use the same route — it speeds up approval. Withdrawals must always go to an account in the name of the account holder.
Why was my withdrawal declined?
The two usual causes are incomplete verification and open bonus wagering. Both must be resolved before a request will be processed.
