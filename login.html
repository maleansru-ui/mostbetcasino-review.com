KYC

Account verification

Verification is mandatory before the first withdrawal and runs once:

  1. Log in and open the personal details section of your profile.
  2. Complete your full name, date of birth and document number.
  3. Upload a passport or ID card, plus a proof of address if requested.
  4. Wait for approval — normally within 48 hours.
The detail that trips most people: the profile data must match the document exactly. A misspelled name or wrong date of birth is the single most common rejection reason, and correcting it afterwards requires a support ticket.
Recovery

Forgot your password?

  1. Click "Forgot your password?" in the login window.
  2. Enter the email or phone number linked to the account.
  3. You receive a reset link by email or a code by SMS.
  4. Set a new password and log in again.

If nothing arrives, check the spam folder and confirm you are using the exact contact you registered with. If that contact no longer exists, support will ask for identity documents instead.

Mirrors and blocked domains
